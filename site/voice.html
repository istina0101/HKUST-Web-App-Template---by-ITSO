Voice & copy

Plain, concrete, institutional. The interface explains what happens and why, in as few words as the fact needs.

Tone

The HKUST brand voice is passionate · intelligent · confident · inspiring · friendly. In an admin app that becomes: direct sentences, real names of systems and people, no hedging, no cheer. The product addresses the user as you and says what the system will do.

Sounds right"Self-service reset replaces the helpdesk ticket. Most resets finish in under a minute once MFA is confirmed."
Sounds wrong"Great news! You can now reset your password super quickly without bothering anyone 🎉"

Punctuation

  • Middle dot · (spaced) joins facts: "Manager · Finance Office", "iPhone · added 12 Jan 2025".
  • Arrow → ends inline links that navigate: "Manage sign-in →". Never "Click here".
  • Ampersand in titles where the pairing is a name: "Home & Apps", "Policy & SoD".
  • No exclamation marks. No emoji. Ellipsis "…" only in placeholders.

Naming

  • First mention: The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology; then HKUST — never "THKUST", never "the HKUST".
  • ITSO = Information Technology Services Office; spell out on first mention.
  • System names as their owners write them: HRMS, ECM, CADS, PeopleSoft SIS, Microsoft 365, ServiceNow.
