Using a template

Copy the foldertemplates/<slug>/ into your project. The .dc.html is the page; ds-base.js points at the system — edit its base line so it finds styles.css and _ds_bundle.js.
Edit copy in placeHeadings, cells and captions are ordinary HTML. Data-driven parts (table rows, chart series) live in the small logic class at the bottom of the file.
Keep the shellBand, sidebar and page head come from the components; change the nav items and titles, not their geometry.
