Do & don't

The rules that go wrong most often, side by side.

Logo & band

DoPut the white HKUST lockup top-left on the navy band at 34px, with the unit name after a 1px 40%-white rule.
Don'tCentre the logo, put it on the right, use the colour logo on navy, or redraw the sundial.

Colour roles

DoNavy for brand surfaces and headings; ITSO blue for the one primary action; gold for eyebrows; orange only for counters.
Don'tNavy buttons, blue headings, gold text paragraphs, orange primary buttons, or purple/maroon/magenta (other HK universities).

Backgrounds

DoPale-grey page, white cards, solid navy covers.
Don'tGradients, photos behind text, patterns, frosted glass.

Cards

DoOne 1px border, 8px radius, 22px padding, no shadow at rest; lift only when the whole card is a link.
Don'tShadows on resting cards, nested cards, coloured left borders, big radii.

Type

DoMulish; the 7-step ramp; 700 for headings/buttons, 600 for labels; sentence case except Title Case for page titles and nav.
Don'tSizes off the ramp (13.5px), ALL CAPS body text, mixed fonts, light weights under 24px.

Status

DoOne Badge per fact, tone by meaning (warning = pending/sensitive, danger = rejected/high, success = active, neutral = completed).
Don'tTwo badges saying the same thing, coloured text without a badge, danger for anything merely important.

Callouts

DoOne per card, explaining a rule and its consequence in one or two sentences.
Don'tStacks of callouts, exclamation marks, callouts as headings.

Icons

DoLucide, 2px stroke, currentColor, 18px default; icon boxes tinted by category.
Don'tEmoji, filled icons, mixed icon sets, icons without labels in navigation.

Buttons

DoOne primary per section; ghost for secondary; link for cancel/dismiss; danger-solid only to confirm destruction.
Don'tTwo primaries side by side, primary Cancel, red outlined buttons for non-destructive actions.

Motion

Do120–200ms eases on colour, opacity and transform.
Don'tSprings, bounces, marquees, animated backgrounds.

Copy

DoMiddle dots between facts · arrows on navigating links · DD Mon YYYY dates.
Don'tPipes, slashes, "Click here", US date formats, exclamation marks.
